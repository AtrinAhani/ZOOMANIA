Cat Care in Dubai

From high-rise apartments to summer heat, caring for a cat in Dubai has its own rhythm. Here is a calm, practical guide to responsible cat ownership in the UAE.

Cats and Dubai lifestyle

Dubai is home to a large, diverse community of cat lovers — and to many apartment-dwelling cats. Indoor living can be safe, enriching and happy when the space is designed with a cat’s instincts in mind.

Choose a cat whose energy suits your home and schedule, plan for care while you travel, and check your building’s or landlord’s pet policy before you adopt.

Apartment-ready

Vertical spaces, window perches and daily play turn a small flat into a full territory.

Balcony safety

Cats can fall from height. Secure balconies and windows with sturdy netting or screens.

Travel plans

Arrange a trusted sitter or a licensed boarding facility well before holidays.

Caring for cats in hot weather

UAE summers can be extreme. Indoor cats are safest, but heat still matters inside the home.

  • Keep it cool

    Keep living spaces comfortably cool and shaded, and give cats cool spots such as tiled floors. Plan for power or AC failures.

  • Water everywhere

    Place several water bowls around the home and refresh them often; wet food adds moisture.

  • No hot cars — ever

    A parked car heats up dangerously within minutes, even with windows open.

  • Hot surfaces

    Balcony tiles and outdoor paving can burn paws in summer.

  • Know heatstroke signs

    Panting, drooling, restlessness, weakness or bright red gums need urgent veterinary care. Flat-faced breeds such as Persians are at higher risk.

  • Sun protection

    Hairless and white-eared cats can burn; limit direct sun through windows.

Adoption and responsible ownership

Adopting gives a cat a second chance and helps reduce the number of stray and abandoned animals. Before you adopt, make sure you can commit for the cat’s whole life — often 15 years or more.

Before you bring a cat home

  • Confirm your housing allows pets
  • Budget for food, litter, vet care and emergencies
  • Prepare a quiet room, litter box, scratching post and hiding place
  • Book a vet visit for a health check, vaccination and microchip registration
  • Plan for travel, moves and long-term care

Dubai animal welfare

Dubai Municipality runs several programmes that support animal welfare and responsible ownership. The highlights below are summarised from official announcements.

Animal Welfare Shelter, Warsan

In July 2026 Dubai Municipality opened an integrated pet shelter at the Birds and Pets Market in Warsan, alongside a digital adoption platform where residents can browse animals and review their health and behaviour profiles.

Dubai Municipality announcement (opens in a new tab)

Trap-Neuter-Return (TNR)

The municipality applies Trap-Neuter-Return — and, where appropriate, Trap-Neuter-Vaccinate-Return — to manage community cat populations humanely.

Dubai Municipality (opens in a new tab)

Ehsan feeding stations

In 2026 Dubai Municipality launched “Ehsan Stations”, AI-powered smart devices that feed stray animals in an organised, sustainable way.

Dubai Municipality announcement (opens in a new tab)

Veterinary services

The municipality’s Veterinary Services Section provides vaccination, registration and adoption services and oversees veterinary standards in the emirate.

Veterinary Services Section (opens in a new tab)

Rules and resources

A short overview of what pet owners commonly need. Requirements and fees can change, so always confirm details with Dubai Municipality or a licensed veterinary clinic.

Microchip
Pet cats and dogs in Dubai need an ISO-compliant microchip linked to the owner’s details in Dubai Municipality’s veterinary records.
Rabies vaccination
An up-to-date rabies vaccination record is required for registration.
Registration
Cats and dogs must be registered with Dubai Municipality. Registration can be completed through licensed veterinary clinics or the municipality’s online services, and is typically renewed alongside the yearly rabies vaccination.
Keep details current
If you move or change your phone number, update your pet’s registration so a found pet can be returned to you.
Animal welfare law
UAE Federal Law No. 16 of 2007 on animal welfare (as amended) protects animals from abuse and neglect, and abandoning a pet is prohibited.

Dubai cat care FAQ

Do I need to register my cat in Dubai?

Yes. Dubai Municipality requires pet cats and dogs to be microchipped, vaccinated against rabies and registered. A licensed vet clinic can guide you through the process.

Where can I adopt a cat in Dubai?

Dubai Municipality offers adoption through its website and the DubaiNow app, and its Warsan shelter cares for cats awaiting homes. Licensed local rescue groups also rehome cats.

Can my cat live happily indoors in an apartment?

Yes — with daily play, climbing spaces, scratching posts, window views and secure balconies, indoor cats can live full, healthy lives.

How do I keep my cat safe in summer?

Keep your home cool, offer plenty of water, never leave a cat in a car and learn the signs of heatstroke. Contact a vet immediately if you are worried.

What should I do if I find a stray or injured cat?

Keep a safe distance if the cat seems frightened, and contact Dubai Municipality (800 900) or a veterinary clinic for advice. A vet can scan for a microchip to find the owner.

Should I neuter my cat?

For most pet cats, yes. Neutering prevents unplanned litters and supports Dubai’s efforts to reduce stray populations. Ask your vet about timing.
