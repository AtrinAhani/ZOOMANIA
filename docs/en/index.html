Meet Atrin

The person behind ZOOMANIA

Hi, I’m Atrin! 👋

I’m currently a Foundation Year student at Heriot-Watt University, studying toward Engineering Computer Science. ZOOMANIA is my very first website project.

I’ve always been drawn to computers and the creative possibilities of technology, and I also love cats. This website is where those interests meet: a welcoming place to discover different cat breeds, explore a fun personality quiz, and learn about thoughtful, responsible cat care.

As I learn more about building websites, I hope to keep improving ZOOMANIA — one idea, one design, and one line of code at a time.

— Atrin, founder of ZOOMANIA
